Class funds

Collection periods are added manually by admins. Admins and officers can enter each student's total received; payments cover the oldest periods first and carry forward as advance credit. Payment records are visible only to admins and officers.

Fund and collection period tools

Deleting a fund permanently removes its periods and payment records. The Class Fund cannot be deleted.

The workbook follows the class roster layout with student name, number, weekly status, and advance payment.
